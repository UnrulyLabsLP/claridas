It still lost, and it never led — not for a single possession. Seattle cut a fourth-quarter deficit to 83-82 on a Natisha Hiedeman 25-foot three with 6.6 seconds left, but a Portland free throw pushed it back to 84-82, and Hiedeman's 20-foot jumper in the final second missed. The gap sits in one column: two-point shooting. Portland made 19 of 35 two-pointers (54%); Seattle made 13 of 42 (31%). Seattle attempted seven more twos and made six fewer. Score the game by shot type and it closes to the point: Portland scored 38 on twos, 36 on threes, 10 at the line; Seattle scored 26, 42, and 14. That is Portland +12 on two-pointers, Seattle +6 on threes and +4 on free throws — and 12 minus 6 minus 4 is the final margin. Portland scored 38 points in the paint to Seattle's 24. The turnover column, read closely, ran the other way from the loss: Seattle turned the ball over less often (12 to 15) and scored more off those giveaways, 28 to 12. Holly Winterburn led Portland with 20 off the bench; Bridget Carleton, Carla Leite, and Megan DiLeo added 14 apiece. Rookie Awa Fam led Seattle with 20 off its own bench, and Jordan Horston posted 12 points and 11 rebounds. The win completed a four-game season sweep of Seattle by the first-year franchise.
