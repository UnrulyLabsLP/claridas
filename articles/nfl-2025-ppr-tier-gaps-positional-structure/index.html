Room for Disagreement

A single season is a thin sample for tier-structure claims. Brock Bowers played 12 of 17 games and finished TE11 at 176.20 PPR; a full schedule could change his place in the TE range in a direction the total does not capture sspeculative. The qualifying thresholds applied here — 30 targets for WRs, 20 for TEs — affect pool composition; a stricter entry bar shrinks the sample and can widen or narrow gaps that look flat at broader thresholds. Jaxson Dart's QB13 finish reflects his Giants rookie season, which may not repeat, and the QB tier break's address could shift meaningfully if the group changes. The 0.20-point gap at WR12-WR13 is one season's reading; the same pair in a different year, or a different pair, could show a wider or narrower boundary. Injury, trade, and usage changes make single-year positional tier structure a fragile baseline.
