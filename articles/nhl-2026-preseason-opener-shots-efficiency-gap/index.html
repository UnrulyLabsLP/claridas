We read 8 NHL preseason games: every home team won — and 3 did it with fewer shots

Colorado put 32 shots on net and scored once — 3 home teams won despite being outshot. The power play managed 5 goals on 47 chances, none at all in 5 of the 8 games.

Filed by the Claridas sports pod · September 22, 2026

The Facts

We read the 8 games ESPN listed as Final when we pulled the scoreboard on the morning of September 22, 2026 — all of them played Monday, September 21, early in the NHL preseason. Eight games, eight home-team wins. Across those 8 games: 398 total shots on net, 37 goals, a combined shooting percentage of 9.3%. In 5 of the 8, the home team also outshot its opponent and won. In the other three, the home team had fewer shots and still won — each time because it scored on a bigger share of them: Colorado Avalanche at Winnipeg Jets — Colorado outshot Winnipeg 32 to 19. Winnipeg won 3–1. Colorado scored on 1 of its 32 shots (3.1%); Winnipeg scored on 3 of 19 (15.8%). Minnesota Wild at Chicago Blackhawks — Minnesota outshot Chicago 22 to 20. Chicago won 4–3. Minnesota scored on 3 of 22 shots (13.6%); Chicago scored on 4 of 20 (20.0%). St. Louis Blues at Dallas Stars — St. Louis outshot Dallas 31 to 27. Dallas won 2–1. St. Louis scored on 1 of 31 shots (3.2%); Dallas scored on 2 of 27 (7.4%). In each of the three wins on fewer shots, the home team scored on a bigger share of its shots — by at least four percentage points. Power play — the stretch after a penalty when one team skates a man up: the 16 teams drew 47 such chances and scored five times, a 10.6% rate. Five of the 8 games ended with neither team scoring on the power play. The three games that produced any power-play goals: Washington vs. Philadelphia (2 goals on 6 chances), Pittsburgh vs. Buffalo (2 goals on 5 chances), Montreal vs. Ottawa (1 goal on 10 chances).
