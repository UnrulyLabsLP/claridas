The Facts
Two teams that played their first WNBA game this season met Friday night at Rogers Arena in Vancouver, and the one that shot better from three lost. The Portland Fire made 11 three-pointers to the Toronto Tempo's 6, took more shots (70 to 59), and led for 61% of the game, by as many as 13 points. Toronto won 82-79. The game divides into three clean columns. Portland won the three-point column by 15 points — 11 makes to 6. Toronto won the two-point column by 6 (19 makes to 16) and the free-throw column by 12 (26 to 14). Six plus twelve minus fifteen is three — the final margin. The free-throw column is the story. Toronto attempted 33 free throws to Portland's 17 — nearly twice as many — and made 26 to Portland's 14. Isabelle Harrison, Toronto's leading scorer with 25 points, did not make a three-pointer: she went 0 for 3 from the arc and 9 for 10 at the line. Toronto's top two scorers, Harrison and Kiki Rice (19 points), combined for 44 points and one three-pointer between them. Portland's offense pointed the other way — Bridget Carleton alone made five threes and scored 18. After Portland took a 76-75 lead on a 13-0 run with 3:28 to play, Toronto retook the lead on the last two field goals of the game — a Harrison layup for 77-76, then a Rice layup for 79-76 — and closed it out at the line, where Harrison sealed it with two free throws. The win snapped a 12-game Toronto losing streak that ran from July 14 through August 19.
