Games, not news · The arcade

Stacks

A block-carrying puzzle. Walk, climb one tile at a time, and pick up blocks to build the steps you need to reach the door. No timer and no way to lose — a stuck board is undone or restarted. This is a pack, not a daily: work through it at your own pace.

  1. Level 1 · First steps Walk to the door.
  2. Level 2 · Up one Face a wall one tile high and climb it.
  3. Level 3 · Two steps Climb a staircase one tile at a time.
  4. Level 4 · Grab Pick up the block in front of you, then set it down.
  5. Level 5 · One block up Carry the block to the wall, set it down as a step, then climb over.
  6. Level 6 · Carry across Bring the block the long way to build your step at the wall.
  7. Level 7 · Two high One block is not enough. Build a two-step staircase to clear the tall wall.
  8. Level 8 · Three steps A taller wall needs a taller staircase — three blocks, three steps.
  9. Level 9 · Two walls Two low walls, two blocks. Spend each one where it counts.
  10. Level 10 · Reach The blocks are far from the wall. Ferry them over one at a time.
  11. Level 11 · Long haul A tall wall and a long walk. Plan the staircase before you start.
  12. Level 12 · Over and down Climb the wall; the door sits low on the far side.
  13. Level 13 · Higher still A three-tile wall with the blocks spread out. Gather them at the wall.
  14. Level 14 · In two acts Clear the first wall, then build again for the second.
  15. Level 15 · Staircase Four tiles high. You will need every block and a full staircase.
  16. Level 16 · Ferry The blocks sit past the wall's foot. Bring each one back into place.
  17. Level 17 · Step and over A step up, then a wall to scale. Read the whole board first.
  18. Level 18 · The climb The hardest yet: a four-tile wall and a long approach.
  19. Level 19 · Long approach Three blocks by your feet, a tall wall far away. Carry them the distance.
  20. Level 20 · Two gates Two low walls, two blocks. Spend each one where it counts.
  21. Level 21 · High and far A four-tile wall with the blocks spread down the room.
  22. Level 22 · Two steps up A wall, a gap, and a second wall. Read where each block has to land.
  23. Level 23 · The tower Five tiles high. Four blocks, one long staircase, no room for waste.
  24. Level 24 · Switchback Climb, cross, and climb again — a wall on each side of the room.
  25. Level 25 · The long wall Four tiles high, three blocks, and a long walk. Build the whole staircase.
  26. Level 26 · Twin steps Two walls, one block for each. Do not spend both in the same place.
  27. Level 27 · The great tower Five tiles high. Four blocks, one perfect staircase, nothing to spare.
  28. Level 28 · Mind the gap A pit you cannot climb out of. Set a block down as a bridge before you cross.
  29. Level 29 · Gauntlet Three gates, three blocks. Clear them in a single run.
  30. Level 30 · The last door Everything you have learned: a tall wall, spare blocks, and one way through.
