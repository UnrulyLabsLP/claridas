Games, not news · The arcade

Stacks — Level 5

One block up. Carry the block to the wall, set it down as a step, then climb over.

Arrow keys walk and climb (climbing is one tile at a time). Space or X picks up the block in front of you and sets it down again to build steps. Z undoes, R restarts. No timer, no way to lose — if you get stuck, undo or restart.
