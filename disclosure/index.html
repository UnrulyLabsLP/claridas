Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

How we make money — and the wall between the money and the coverage. Last updated August 16, 2026.

Claridas News runs on advertising and, in some articles, affiliate links. This page states in plain terms how that works and what it does not change. Trust is the only thing we sell, so we show you the arrangement.

Affiliate links — the disclosure

Some articles link to a product or service through an affiliate link. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission. You pay nothing extra. We add such a link only where it fits the topic, and we mark the affiliate module where it appears.

The firewall — money never buys coverage

A commission rate never decides what we cover, what we conclude, or what we recommend. We choose any link after the reporting, never before. We carry no affiliate links and no ads on hard news, on tragedy, on any sensitive story, or on a corrected article. Our recommendations follow the evidence, exactly as our articles do.

Advertising

Any ad is chosen by the page's topic, not by you. Ads carry no behavioral tracking and hold no personal data about you. See our Privacy page for what the site does and does not collect.
