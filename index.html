Federal outlays rose $309 billion through July, yet 19 of 29 agencies spent less — four lines covered the whole increase

On the World Bank's 2021 renewable-energy-share series (212 economies), the number falls as income rises: low-income countries post a median 76.9%, high-income 11.6%.
